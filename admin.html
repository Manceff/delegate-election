Overview

    Candidates

    Live counts, visible only on this page until voting closes. Removing a candidate also removes the votes they received, and those classmates can vote again.

    Possible duplicates

    Names that may belong to the same person: same words in another order, one name inside another, or spellings one or two letters apart. Check before removing anything.

    Voter roll

    Everyone who has voted, oldest first. Removing an entry also removes that vote, and the person can vote again.

    Check against the class list

    Paste the class list, one name per line, to see who hasn’t voted and which voters aren’t on the list. The list stays in this browser.
