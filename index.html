    Notice of election

    The candidate with the most votes becomes our class delegate.

    Who can run
    Any student in the class. Fill in the candidacy form with your full name and a short statement, and your name goes on the ballot straight away.
    How to vote
    Tick one name, write your full name and cast your ballot. One ballot per student; it can’t be changed once cast.
    Secret ballot
    Your name is added to the voter roll so nobody votes twice. Your choice drops into the ballot box separately, and the two are never stored together.
    Result
    The count stays sealed until polls close on Monday 21 September at midnight, Paris time. Results then appear on this page. A tie for first place is settled by a run-off.
